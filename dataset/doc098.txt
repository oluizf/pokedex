Número na Pokédex: #375
Nome: Metang
Tipagem: steel, psychic
Fraquezas: fighting, ground, fire
Descrição: When two BELDUM fuse together, METANG is formed. The brains of the BELDUM are joined by a magnetic nervous system. By linking its brains magnetically, this POKéMON generates strong psychokinetic power.

Número na Pokédex: #376
Nome: Metagross
Tipagem: steel, psychic
Fraquezas: fighting, ground, fire
Descrição: METAGROSS has four brains in total. Combined, the four brains can breeze through difficult calculations faster than a supercomputer. This POKéMON can float in the air by tucking in its four legs.

Número na Pokédex: #377
Nome: Regirock
Tipagem: rock
Fraquezas: fighting, ground, steel, water, grass
Descrição: REGIROCK was sealed away by people long ago. If this POKéMON’s body is damaged in battle, it is said to seek out suitable rocks on its own to repair itself.

Número na Pokédex: #378
Nome: Regice
Tipagem: ice
Fraquezas: fighting, rock, steel, fire
