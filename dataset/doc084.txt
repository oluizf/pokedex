Número na Pokédex: #321
Nome: Wailord
Tipagem: water
Fraquezas: grass, electric
Descrição: WAILORD is the largest of all identified POKéMON up to now. This giant POKéMON swims languorously in the vast open sea, eating massive amounts of food at once with its enormous mouth.

Número na Pokédex: #322
Nome: Numel
Tipagem: fire, ground
Fraquezas: ground, rock, water
Descrição: NUMEL is extremely dull witted - it doesn’t notice being hit. However, it can’t stand hunger for even a second. This POKéMON’s body is a seething cauldron of boiling magma. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #323
Nome: Camerupt
Tipagem: fire, ground
Fraquezas: ground, rock, water
Descrição: CAMERUPT has a volcano inside its body. Magma of 18,000 degrees F courses through its body. Occasionally, the humps on this POKéMON’s back erupt, spewing the superheated magma. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #324
Nome: Torkoal
Tipagem: fire
Fraquezas: ground, rock, water
Descrição: TORKOAL digs through mountains in search of coal. If it finds some, it fills hollow spaces on its shell with the coal and burns it. If it is attacked, this POKéMON spouts thick black smoke to beat a retreat.
