Número na Pokédex: #309
Nome: Electrike
Tipagem: electric
Fraquezas: ground
Descrição: ELECTRIKE stores electricity in its long body hair. This POKéMON stimulates its leg muscles with electric charges. These jolts of power give its legs explosive acceleration performance.

Número na Pokédex: #310
Nome: Manectric
Tipagem: electric
Fraquezas: ground
Descrição: MANECTRIC is constantly discharging electricity from its mane. The sparks sometimes ignite forest fires. When it enters a battle, this POKéMON creates thunderclouds.

Número na Pokédex: #311
Nome: Plusle
Tipagem: electric
Fraquezas: ground
Descrição: PLUSLE always acts as a cheerleader for its partners. Whenever a teammate puts out a good effort in battle, this POKéMON shorts out its body to create the crackling noises of sparks to show its joy.

Nome: Minun
Tipagem: electric
