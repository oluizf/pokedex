Nome: Loudred
Tipagem: normal
Fraquezas: fighting
Descrição: LOUDRED’s bellowing can completely decimate a wood-frame house. It uses its voice to punish its foes. This POKéMON’s round ears serve as loudspeakers.

Número na Pokédex: #295
Nome: Exploud
Tipagem: normal
Fraquezas: fighting
Descrição: EXPLOUD triggers earthquakes with the tremors it creates by bellowing. If this POKéMON violently inhales from the ports on its body, it’s a sign that it is preparing to let loose a huge bellow.

Número na Pokédex: #296
Nome: Makuhita
Tipagem: fighting
Fraquezas: flying, psychic, fairy
Descrição: MAKUHITA is tenacious - it will keep getting up and attacking its foe however many times it is knocked down. Every time it gets back up, this POKéMON stores more energy in its body for evolving.
