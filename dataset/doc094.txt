Número na Pokédex: #359
Nome: Absol
Tipagem: dark
Fraquezas: fighting, bug, fairy
Descrição: Every time ABSOL appears before people, it is followed by a disaster such as an earthquake or a tidal wave. As a result, it came to be known as the disaster POKéMON. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #360
Nome: Wynaut
Tipagem: psychic
Fraquezas: bug, ghost, dark
Descrição: WYNAUT can always be seen with a big, happy smile on its face. Look at its tail to determine if it is angry. When angered, this POKéMON will be slapping the ground with its tail. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #361
Nome: Snorunt
Tipagem: ice
Fraquezas: fighting, rock, steel, fire
Descrição: SNORUNT live in regions with heavy snowfall. In seasons without snow, such as spring and summer, this POKéMON steals away to live quietly among stalactites and stalagmites deep in caverns. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #362
Nome: Glalie
Tipagem: ice
Fraquezas: fighting, rock, steel, fire
Descrição: GLALIE has a body made of rock, which it hardens with an armor of ice. This POKéMON has the ability to freeze moisture in the atmosphere into any shape it desires.
