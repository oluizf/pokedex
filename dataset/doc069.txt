Nome: Zigzagoon
Tipagem: normal

Nome: Linoone
Tipagem: normal
Fraquezas: fighting
Descrição: LINOONE always runs full speed and only in straight lines. If facing an obstacle, it makes a right-angle turn to evade it. This POKéMON is very challenged by gently curving roads.

Número na Pokédex: #265
Nome: Wurmple
Tipagem: bug
Fraquezas: flying, rock, fire
Descrição: Using the spikes on its rear end, WURMPLE peels the bark off trees and feeds on the sap that oozes out. This POKéMON’s feet are tipped with suction pads that allow it to cling to glass without slipping.
