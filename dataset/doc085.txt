Número na Pokédex: #325
Nome: Spoink
Tipagem: psychic
Fraquezas: bug, ghost, dark
Descrição: SPOINK bounces around on its tail. The shock of its bouncing makes its heart pump. As a result, this POKéMON cannot afford to stop bouncing - if it stops, its heart will stop.

Número na Pokédex: #326
Nome: Grumpig
Tipagem: psychic
Fraquezas: bug, ghost, dark
Descrição: GRUMPIG uses the black pearls on its body to amplify its psychic power waves for gaining total control over its foe. When this POKéMON uses its special power, its snorting breath grows labored. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #327
Nome: Spinda
Tipagem: normal
Fraquezas: fighting
Descrição: All the SPINDA that exist in the world are said to have utterly unique spot patterns. The shaky, tottering steps of this POKéMON give it the appearance of dancing. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #328
Nome: Trapinch
Tipagem: ground
Fraquezas: water, grass, ice
Descrição: TRAPINCH’s nest is a sloped, bowl-like pit dug in sand. This POKéMON patiently waits for prey to tumble down the pit. Its giant jaws have enough strength to crush even boulders.
