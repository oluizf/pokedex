Número na Pokédex: #271
Nome: Lombre
Tipagem: water, grass
Fraquezas: grass, electric
Descrição: LOMBRE is nocturnal - it will get active after dusk. It is also a mischief-maker. When this POKéMON spots anglers, it tugs on their fishing lines from beneath the surface and enjoys their consternation. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #272
Nome: Ludicolo
Tipagem: water, grass
Fraquezas: grass, electric
Descrição: LUDICOLO begins dancing as soon as it hears cheerful, festive music. This POKéMON is said to appear when it hears the singing of children on hiking outings. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #273
Nome: Seedot
Tipagem: grass
Fraquezas: flying, poison, bug, fire, ice
Descrição: SEEDOT attaches itself to a tree branch using the top of its head. It sucks moisture from the tree while hanging off the branch. The more water it drinks, the glossier this POKéMON’s body becomes. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #274
Nome: Nuzleaf
Tipagem: grass, dark
Fraquezas: flying, poison, bug, fire, ice
Descrição: NUZLEAF live in densely overgrown forests. They occasionally venture out of the forest to startle people. This POKéMON dislikes having its long nose pinched.
