Número na Pokédex: #317
Nome: Swalot
Tipagem: poison
Fraquezas: ground, psychic
Descrição: When SWALOT spots prey, it spurts out a hideously toxic fluid from its pores and sprays the target. Once the prey has weakened, this POKéMON gulps it down whole with its cavernous mouth. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #318
Nome: Carvanha
Tipagem: water, dark
Fraquezas: grass, electric
Descrição: CARVANHA’s strongly developed jaws and its sharply pointed fangs pack the destructive power to rip out boat hulls. Many boats have been attacked and sunk by this POKéMON. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #319
Nome: Sharpedo
Tipagem: water, dark
Fraquezas: grass, electric
Descrição: Nicknamed “the bully of the sea,” SHARPEDO is widely feared. Its cruel fangs grow back immediately if they snap off. Just one of these POKéMON can thoroughly tear apart a supertanker. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #320
Nome: Wailmer
Tipagem: water
Fraquezas: grass, electric
Descrição: WAILMER’s nostrils are located above its eyes. This playful POKéMON loves to startle people by forcefully snorting out seawater it stores inside its body out of its nostrils. 
----------------------------------------
