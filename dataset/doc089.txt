Nome: Whiscash
Tipagem: water, ground
Fraquezas: grass, electric
Descrição: WHISCASH is extremely territorial. Just one of these POKéMON will claim a large pond as its exclusive territory. If a foe approaches it, it thrashes about and triggers a massive earthquake.

Nome: Corphish
Tipagem: water
Fraquezas: grass, electric
Descrição: CORPHISH were originally foreign POKéMON that were imported as pets. They eventually turned up in the wild. This POKéMON is very hardy and has greatly increased its population.

Nome: Crawdaunt
Tipagem: water, dark
Fraquezas: grass, electric
Descrição: CRAWDAUNT has an extremely violent nature that compels it to challenge other living things to battle. Other life-forms refuse to live in ponds inhabited by this POKéMON, making them desolate places.
