Nome: Kecleon
Tipagem: normal
Fraquezas: fighting
Descrição: KECLEON is capable of changing its body colors at will to blend in with its surroundings. There is one exception - this POKéMON can’t change the zigzag pattern on its belly.

Número na Pokédex: #353
Nome: Shuppet
Tipagem: ghost
Fraquezas: ghost, dark
Descrição: SHUPPET is attracted by feelings of jealousy and vindictiveness. If someone develops strong feelings of vengeance, this POKéMON will appear in a swarm and line up beneath the eaves of that person’s home.
