Número na Pokédex: #267
Nome: Beautifly
Tipagem: bug, flying
Fraquezas: flying, rock, fire
Descrição: BEAUTIFLY’s favorite food is the sweet pollen of flowers. If you want to see this POKéMON, just leave a potted flower by an open window. BEAUTIFLY is sure to come looking for pollen.

Número na Pokédex: #268
Nome: Cascoon
Tipagem: bug
Fraquezas: flying, rock, fire
Descrição: CASCOON makes its protective cocoon by wrapping its body entirely with a fine silk from its mouth. Once the silk goes around its body, it hardens. This POKéMON prepares for its evolution inside the cocoon. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #269
Nome: Dustox
Tipagem: bug, poison
Fraquezas: flying, rock, fire
Descrição: DUSTOX is instinctively drawn to light. Swarms of this POKéMON are attracted by the bright lights of cities, where they wreak havoc by stripping the leaves off roadside trees for food. 
----------------------------------------

Número na Pokédex: #270
Nome: Lotad
Tipagem: water, grass
Fraquezas: grass, electric
Descrição: LOTAD live in ponds and lakes, where they float on the surface. It grows weak if its broad leaf dies. On rare occasions, this POKéMON travels on land in search of clean water. 
----------------------------------------
